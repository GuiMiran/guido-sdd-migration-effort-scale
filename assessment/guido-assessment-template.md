# GUIDO Framework Assessment Template  
## Organizational Readiness Evaluation for Specification-Driven Development (SDD)

**Framework:** GUIDO Scale™  
**Author:** Guido Miranda Mercado  
**Version:** 1.0  

---

## Purpose

This assessment template provides a structured method to evaluate an organization's maturity and migration effort toward **Specification-Driven Development (SDD)** in AI-agentic software engineering environments.

The assessment measures five key pillars that determine readiness for AI-driven, specification-centric development.

---

## Assessment Instructions

Each question must be scored on a scale from:

| Score | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| 1 | Not present / very weak |
| 2 | Initial / inconsistent |
| 3 | Defined / partially implemented |
| 4 | Well established |
| 5 | Fully mature / optimized |

Total possible score: **25 points**

---

## Pillar 1 — Documentation & Knowledge Structure

### Objective
Evaluate the organization's ability to maintain structured, accessible, and actionable documentation.

### Questions

1. Are software requirements formally documented and maintained?
2. Is architectural documentation standardized and up to date?
3. Are specifications traceable across the development lifecycle?
4. Is documentation actively used during development processes?
5. Are documentation practices integrated into CI/CD workflows?

---

## Pillar 2 — Process Discipline & Governance

### Objective
Assess process standardization and engineering governance maturity.

### Questions

1. Are development processes clearly defined and standardized?
2. Are roles and responsibilities formally documented?
3. Are quality gates enforced consistently?
4. Is compliance monitored systematically?
5. Are change management processes structured and documented?

---

## Pillar 3 — Automation & Toolchain Maturity

### Objective
Evaluate engineering automation and technical infrastructure readiness.

### Questions

1. Are CI/CD pipelines implemented and consistently used?
2. Is automated testing widely adopted?
3. Are deployment processes standardized?
4. Are monitoring and observability practices in place?
5. Are infrastructure processes automated?

---

## Pillar 4 — AI Readiness & Agent Integration

### Objective
Measure preparedness for AI-assisted software development.

### Questions

1. Is AI currently used in development workflows?
2. Are AI tools integrated into engineering processes?
3. Is there governance for AI-generated artifacts?
4. Are AI-related risks managed systematically?
5. Is there experience with agent orchestration or AI automation?

---

## Pillar 5 — Cultural & Organizational Readiness

### Objective
Assess cultural readiness for transformation toward AI-driven engineering.

### Questions

1. Are teams open to adopting AI technologies?
2. Is there leadership support for AI transformation?
3. Are training programs available for AI skills?
4. Is knowledge sharing encouraged across teams?
5. Are teams adaptable to process changes?

---

## Scoring Model

### Total Score Calculation

Sum all pillar scores.

Maximum: 25 points.

---

## GUIDO Level Classification

| Score Range | GUIDO Level |
|-------------|-------------|
| 5 – 8 | GUIDO 1 — Chaotic State |
| 9 – 12 | GUIDO 2 — Initial Directed |
| 13 – 16 | GUIDO 3 — Defined Standards |
| 17 – 20 | GUIDO 4 — Quantitatively Managed |
| 21 – 25 | GUIDO 5 — SDD-Native |

---

## Interpretation Guidelines

### GUIDO 1–2
High migration effort expected. Organizations require foundational improvements in documentation and governance.

### GUIDO 3
Moderate effort. Organizations have sufficient maturity to begin structured SDD adoption.

### GUIDO 4–5
Low migration effort. Organizations are ready for AI-native engineering workflows.
