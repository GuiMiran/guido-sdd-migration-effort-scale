# GUIDO Scale™ — Executive Scorecard  
## Rapid Assessment for Specification-Driven Development Readiness

**Framework:** GUIDO Scale  
**Author:** Guido Miranda Mercado  
**Version:** 1.0  

---

## Purpose

The GUIDO Executive Scorecard provides a quick, high-level evaluation of an organization's readiness to transition toward Specification-Driven Development (SDD) in AI-agentic engineering environments.

This scorecard is designed for:

- CTOs
- Engineering Leaders
- Digital Transformation Executives
- Enterprise Architects

---

## How to Use

Rate each category from **1 to 5**:

| Score | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| 1 | Not present |
| 2 | Emerging |
| 3 | Defined |
| 4 | Mature |
| 5 | Optimized |

---

## Scorecard Dimensions

### 1. Documentation Readiness

Evaluate the organization's ability to maintain structured and actionable specifications.

Score:

- Requirements are formally documented
- Architecture documentation is standardized
- Documentation is actively maintained
- Specifications guide development decisions

**Score:** ___ / 5

---

### 2. Process Discipline

Assess process consistency and governance maturity.

Score:

- Development processes are standardized
- Roles and responsibilities are clearly defined
- Quality gates are enforced
- Change management is structured

**Score:** ___ / 5

---

### 3. Automation Maturity

Evaluate the level of engineering automation.

Score:

- CI/CD pipelines are implemented
- Automated testing coverage is high
- Deployment processes are standardized
- Monitoring and observability exist

**Score:** ___ / 5

---

### 4. AI Integration Readiness

Measure preparedness for AI-driven development.

Score:

- AI tools are used in development workflows
- Teams understand AI capabilities
- Governance exists for AI-generated artifacts
- AI is integrated into engineering pipelines

**Score:** ___ / 5

---

### 5. Organizational Culture Readiness

Assess cultural adaptability to transformation.

Score:

- Leadership supports AI initiatives
- Teams are open to change
- Training programs exist
- Knowledge sharing is encouraged

**Score:** ___ / 5

## GUIDO Level Classification

| Score | GUIDO Level | Migration Effort |
|------|-------------|-----------------|
| 5 – 8 | GUIDO 1 | Very High |
| 9 – 12 | GUIDO 2 | High |
| 13 – 16 | GUIDO 3 | Moderate |
| 17 – 20 | GUIDO 4 | Low |
| 21 – 25 | GUIDO 5 | Minimal |

---

## Interpretation Guide

### GUIDO 1–2
Organizations require foundational improvements in documentation and governance before adopting SDD.

### GUIDO 3
Organizations can begin structured adoption with moderate effort.

### GUIDO 4–5
Organizations are ready for AI-native, specification-centric engineering.
